      CHAOS HAS ITS LIMITS

      The house rules.

      Two weapon positions, capacity 5—or 6 with Quartermaster. Eight shared equipment slots. One of each tool. Up to four consumables in each category: throwables, placeables, shots and Tarot cards.

      Every item is bought at full price. All standard weapon unlocks are assumed available; equipment purchases respect your Bloodline rank and spending limit. Scarce, event-only, removed and unverified gear cannot roll.

      Both modes cap melee tools at two, including throwing knives, axes and spears. Melee weapons do not count toward this app preference. Playable chaos adds a First Aid Kit and a melee option. Full chaos removes those two preferences. Both modes keep the game rules. A large weapon may leave your second weapon position empty.

      Go Crazy favours eccentric weapons, traps, decoys and utility gear, and allows repeated consumables more readily. It ignores flavor and trait weighting and does not guarantee healing or melee. Rank, budget, holds and equipment limits still apply. Mulligans (M) have no usage cap while items remain. Each rerolls an item and removes one random item from that hunter, including held gear or the replacement. Each tool or consumable has ten times the removal weight of each weapon. Loadout Mulligan redraws only occupied positions, then removes 1 random item on your first use, 2 on your second, 3 on your third, and so on. Each hunter has their own counter, saved with the squad. You cannot use it if you have fewer remaining items than the next penalty. All losses remain empty until a fresh squad deal, which resets the counters. Losses can remove held gear, healing, melee or challenge weapons. Release a held item before using its item mulligan.

      Optional custom ammo covers 132 weapon variants and supported dual-wield pairs. Every ammo slot rolls separately, including split reserves and bullet/shotgun barrels. Each eligible slot has an 85% custom ammo chance before budget restrictions. All ammo prices count toward the spending limit. Enable it only if you have unlocked the listed ammo; scarce and retired types are excluded. Trait switches describe equipment you already have on this hunter, with a maximum of 15 selected.

      Squad options

      Mild keeps a medkit and melee option. Unhinged favors unusual gear. Cursed strongly favors eccentric weapons and repeated consumables. Intensity applies to Go Crazy mode.

      Assign sniper, close range and support in hunter order, or pick a role below each name. Sniper and close range constrain the primary weapon; support favors healing, resupply and choke gear. Challenges are hard rules: conflicting roles or held items need to be changed.

      Roulette deals a randomly ordered challenge that fits the squad. Incompatible challenges are skipped; if none fit, your current squad stays unchanged. Unique weapons applies to squad, hunter and slot rolls.

      Rolls every ammo slot separately across 132 weapon variants and supported dual pairs, including split reserves and combination barrels.
