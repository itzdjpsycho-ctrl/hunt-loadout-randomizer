Description

A shot that continually restores health over a long duration.

Recommended Traits

The Regeneration Shot can use the following Traits:

  • Vigor - While in Dark Sight, doubles the rate at which Health and Stamina regenerate.

Use Tips

Useful for extended gunfights or when fighting bosses to save your main healing tools when it's needed. Can also help shrug off light damage and bleeding quickly to prevent high damage guns from taking you out as Long Ammo guns such as the Sparks can kill players easily with only minor amounts of missing health at medium and even longer ranges with a chest shot.

REGENERATION SHOT. (See also, FIELD MEDICINE, STIMULANTS). A syringe-administered stimulant which offers immediate relief, claiming to affect the body's natural healing processes and spurring them into overdrive. This makes the effects of the injection less instantaneous than other types of vitality injections, with the benefit of being potent over time.
