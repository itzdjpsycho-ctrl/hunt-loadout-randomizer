Handling

What firing does to your view

The muzzle climbs 45° after every shot. How much of the screen that eats is not fixed - it depends on how wide your own field of view is, so these are drawn at the game's default 55°, and a wider setting costs you less of it.

  • The kick itself plays out in 0.17s, long over before you can fire again at 0.6s.
  • It walks 2° to the right as well as up.
  • Its kick is steeper than 100% of 154 pistol loadouts.

Where the shot goes

Your weapon rests shouldered, so that is the cone an unaimed shot uses - crouching, walking and jumping all scale it. Sighted, it closes to 0.7.

  • 10 Jumping ×2
  • 7.5 Walking ×1.5
  • 5 Shouldered
  • 2.5 Crouched ×0.5
  • 0.7 Aiming
  • Its shouldered cone is better than 8% of 154 pistol loadouts.
  • Jumping throws a sighted shot away entirely - the cone opens from 0.7 to 2100.
