Description

On detonation emits a persistent, deadly cloud. Anything that passes through or gets caught in the cloud will be poisoned.

Effect

Can be thrown in an arc. Breaks on impact, dealing 13 damage and inflicting poison in a 3 meter radius, and creating a poison cloud with a radius of 3 meters that lasts for 510 seconds (8.5 minutes).

Recommended Traits

The Poison Bomb can use the following Traits:

  • Pitcher - Increased throwing range for all items using the aim helper.

Use Tips

Poison Bombs exccel as aera denial tools by throwing them into doorways, on hunter bodies or windows. They are quite cheap but can often be more easily circumvented than other aera denial consummables as there is multiple perks and tools that can help players deal with the clouds.

Book of Weapons

POISON BOMB. (See also, EXPLOSIVES, TOXINS) An experimental device, at the pinnacle of the warfare technology of its time. A compressed gas is contained in a glass vial, which upon release expands rapidly. This gas is composed of a compound of toxic and noxious chemicals engineered to damage human physiology. The exact composition will not be reproduced here for various reasons. First: the exact composition varies from device to device as, Second: the experimental nature of the gas was devised by competing chemists who were careful to conceal their recipes as, Third: distribution of the recipe could allow the gas to be reproduced and used for nefarious purposes. A small explosive charge on a detonator ensures the vial is broken on use, as well as partially accelerating the distribution of the gas.
