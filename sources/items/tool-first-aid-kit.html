Description

A bag of medical supplies that heal 50 health and stop bleeding. Can be used three times.

Recommended Traits

The First Aid Kit can use the following Traits:

  • Doctor - Doubles the amount of Health restored by First Aid Kits.
  • Physician - Reduce the time needed to bandage.

Use Tips

The First Aid Kit is an essential tool for any hunter loadout and should largely be automatically included as it provides an effective consistant way to heal from damage only reinforced when perks such as Doctor and Physician come into play.

FIRST AID KIT. (See also, FIELD MEDICINE) No responsible hunter or soldier should ever be in the field without a first aid kit, as there is much that can go wrong with firearms, even when used properly, including burns (known as "stippling"), ballistic trauma, and death. For a quick field dressing, wrap the wound with bandages tightly, to keep out dirt and staunch further bleeding.
