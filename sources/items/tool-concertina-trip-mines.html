Description

This portable tripwire mine deploys a small concertina bundle perfect for blocking doors and windows. Comes in a pack of two.

Effect

Can be placed in the world. When tripped, creates a small bundle of concertina wire.

Like all trip mines, it can be picked up by interacting with it before it has tripped. Picking it up restores a charge of this tool, if the Hunter was missing one. If the Hunter has full charges or doesn't have this tool equipped, picking it up allows it to be placed elsewhere or harmlessly dropped on the ground.

Recommended Traits

The Concertina Trip Mines can use the following Traits:

  • Poacher - Place and disarm traps quietly.
  • Vigilant - Nearby traps and active beetles are highlighted in Dark Sight. Also applies to Beetle view. (25m)

Use Tips

Concertina trips are deadly when stacked with other placable traps such as Poison Trip Mines allowing you to kill players and prevent them from being rescued\revived in any reasonable time as the lingering poison and wire forces players to take time to remove it and wait for the poison cloud to vanish. they are also excellent for blocking access to a doorway or aera once triggered or even trapping the victim unable to leave back the door they came in without removing the wire.

Book of Weapons

CONCERTINA TRIP MINE. (See also, TACTICAL DEVICES) The concertina trip mine is a more malicious and powerful development of the non-lethal version of the device patented by Samuel McCollin in 1879. Featuring an explosive spool of concertina wire, on being tripped the device expands rapidly, snaring the victim, or obstructing a choke point. Rather than a deterrent, its function is far more offensive. A trespasser, once having tripped the wire, is forced to find a way out before the trap's setter finds them, for surely their intentions would be deadly. Similarly to the tamer alert trip mine, the concertina trip mine did not see mass production, but was passed from individual to individual as a schematic design that could be built at home.
