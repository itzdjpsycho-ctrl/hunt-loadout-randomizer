Description

Crossbow that fires powerful bolts. Quiet at range, but cumbersome with a long reload. Bolts can be recovered and reused. Modified with a mid-range scope.

Recommended Traits

The Crossbow can use the following Traits:

  • Blade Seer - Bolts, Arrows, Throwing Axes, Throwing Knives, and Throwing Spears are highlighted in Dark Sight for better visibility. Also applies to Stalker Beetle View. (25m)
  • Bolt Thrower - Reduced reload time for Crossbows, Bomb Launchers, and Bomb Lances.
  • Steady Aim - Weapon sway gradually lessens when you're looking through a scope or Aperture sights.
