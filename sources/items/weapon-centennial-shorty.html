Description

Winfield made, lever-action repeating rifle. High muzzle velocity and good damage at range. Shortened stock and barrel.

Recommended Traits

The Centennial Shorty can use the following Traits:

  • Iron Eye - Remain in iron sights between shots when firing bolt-action, lever-action, and pump-action weapons.
  • Levering - Faster rate of fire from the hip (First aim stage) when using lever-action weapons.
  • Quartermaster - Gain +1 Weapon Capacity
