Description

Specter Arms made, repeating rifle. Well-balanced and fast reloads make this effective at medium range, but lacks Levering.

Recommended Traits

The 1865 Carbine can use the following Traits:

  • Iron Eye - Remain in iron sights between shots.

Ammo Types

FMJ Ammo - 50

  • Drop Range: 115 → 105
  • Vertical Recoil: 4 → 7
  • Muzzle Velocity: 340 → 272
  • Damage dropoff begins at 40m

Subsonic Ammo - 10

  • Extra Ammo: 21 → 25
  • Drop Range: 115 → 95
  • Muzzle Velocity: 340 → 242
  • Reduced Sound

Trivia

  • The 1865 Carbine's real life counterpart is the Spencer Repeating Rifle.
  • The barrel shank has text stamped into it, reading "MODEL 1865 SPECTER REPEATING RIFLE PAT D APRIL 19 1863 MANUED AT PROV.. R.I BY SHEYNIN RIFLE Co."
