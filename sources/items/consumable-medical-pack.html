Description

A portable medical kit that heals 100 health. Deployable in active health emergencies.

Use Tips

Medical Packs can be useful to support teammates who need healing but have no healing themselfs, espically if your in a compound where supplies are short like Scrapbeak's Lair where items are only dropped by scrapbeak himself. Be wary that in gunfights other players can make use of the pack if it is not fully empty. The Medical Pack can also be a budget alternative to the Fist Aid Kit only costing 15 hunt dollars but only having one use per player like medical packs found on maps.
