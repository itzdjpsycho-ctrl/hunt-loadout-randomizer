Description

Two-handed melee weapon, also balanced for throwing straight and true. Can be retrieved and reused. You'll receive one, be careful how you use it.

Recommended Traits

The Throwing Spear can use the following Traits:

  • Blade Seer - Bolts, Arrows, Throwing Axes, Throwing Knives, and Throwing Spears are highlighted in Dark Sight for better visibility. Also applies to Beetle View. (25m)
  • Silent Killer - Reduces the sound you make when performing melee attacks.

Use Tips

Throwing spears are deadly and can easily kill players at close and even mediumish range though they are pricy and you only get one so it is highly advised players remember to retreive it after use.
