Description

Fire Beetles are bred to find and track prey, giving Hunters a view from above. Upon death or command, they will burst into flames that burn everything they touch.

Recommended Traits

The Fire Beetle can use the following Traits:

  • Blade Seer - Bolts, Arrows, Throwing Axes, Throwing Knives, and Throwing Spears are highlighted in Dark Sight for better visibility. Also applies to Beetle View. (25m)
  • Blast Sense - Loud sounds such as gunshots and explosions are highlighted while in Dark Sight. Gunshots from Silenced Weapons with Subsonic Ammo cannot be seen. (200m)
  • Pain Sense - You can see nearby bleeding, poisoned, and burning Hunters while in Dark Sight.
  • Pitcher - Increased throwing range for all items using the aim helper.
  • Poltergeist - Players can manipulate, interact, and agitate in-world objects and animals remotely via Dark Sight. (50m) Works in Beetle View.
  • Remedy - Using Dark Sight, interact with a Trait Spur within range to trigger a Restoration effect for your team's Hunters. (25m)
  • Serpent - Using Dark Sight, interact with nearby Clues, Rifts, Banishable Targets and abandoned Bounty from a safe distance. (25m)
  • Vigilant - Nearby traps and active beetles are highlighted in Dark Sight. Also applies to Beetle view. (25m)
  • Witness - While in Dark Sight, dead Hunters are Monsters are visible and health restoration is marginally increased when close. Also highlighted in Beetle view.

Use Tips

The Fire Beetle is a very useful tool for reconissance and tracking targets with teammates so calling out and marking is important when in use. Beetles can also benefit from a wide array of perks that help gather information as well as interacting with the enviroment. Fire Beetles are certainly the more offensive of all the other types. capable of setting players on fire, triggering explosives, or aera denial when used in combination of other placed traps or objects.

Book of Weapons

FIRE BEETLE (See also, INSECTS, ORGANICS) Further documents, courtesy of an entomologist now only known as Harriet, indicate the emergence of several members in this new species. Classifying them remains deeply elusive, but we do now possess crucial insights.

The most striking discovery is that most of what this "Harriet" allegedly deduced about these creatures was done by training each individual beetle personally. It is assumed that "melding minds" refers to a close bond between trainer and beetle developed over months of training. It is most curious to note that these beetles must have had dog-like intellect. Alternately, we accept that Harriet was prone to hyperbole. His ego is unquestionably rampant, after all.

He even claims to be close to creating new evolutionary branches of these beetles. I would say he is the height of folly, but in these studies, we have uncovered far bolder God complexes.

Trivia

  • As an April Fool's joke skinned versions of all Beetle types named "Cowboy Beetle" were available from April 1st to April 15th, 2026. Players could buy them like regular Beetles for the same price and there was a redeem code to get ten instances of all three Beetles for free.
