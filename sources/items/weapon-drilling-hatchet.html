Description

German made, break-action combination firearm with two rifle barrels and single shotgun underbarrel. Highly flexible. Shortened and modified with an axe blade.

Recommended Traits

The Drilling Hatchet can use the following Traits:

  • Quartermaster - Gain +1 Weapon Capacity
  • Silent Killer - Reduces the sound you make when performing melee attacks.

Ammo Types

The statistics for the shotgun custom ammo list changes based on the stats of the medium ammo. The true damage and spread stats of the Drilling Hatchet's buckshot are unspecified, but based on its Penny Shot and Slug stats, it may be comparable to the Romero 77 Shorty with around 214 damage and 25 spread.

Book of Weapons

DRILLING HATCHET. (See also, DRILLING, RIFLES, SHOTGUNS) The Drilling as manufactured had great utility for hunting: being a combination gun that could bring down a boar as easily as a duck and keep both carcasses profitable. Shortening the Drilling and adding a hatchet was common among brigands, as it turned the combination gun from an elegant hunting tool into an all-purpose murder weapon.
