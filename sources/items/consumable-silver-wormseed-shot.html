Jump to navigation Jump to search

Silver Wormseed Shot
Price
80
Source
The Harvest Event
Stats
Melee Damage
13
Heavy Melee Damage
27

Description

There's no need to worry about how it crawls in your veins. The Silver Wormseed Shot allows you to gain 25% more Event Points and see 10m farther in Dark Sight. Anything else is just a temporary side effect. Ensure your teammates use it too for combined effect.

Update History

Update Patch Notes
The Harvest Event Silver Wormseed Shot added to the game as an Event consumable.
Update 1.7 Silver Wormseed Shot removed from the game.
