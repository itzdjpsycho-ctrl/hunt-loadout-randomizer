Description

A scoped rifle with high damage and medium ammo. Requires a two-stage reload with the placement of a percussion cap.

Recommended Traits

The Maynard Sniper can use the following Traits:

  • Fast Fingers - Prepare some bullets in hand to reload single-shot rifles, and their non-pistol variants, faster
  • Steady Aim - Weapon sway gradually lessens when you're looking through a scope or Aperture sights

Ammo Types
