Description

Dynamite charge in a sticky frame that can be attached to objects and enemies. Its eight-second fuse doubles the detonation time of a dynamite stick.

Information

  • Despite the listed damage of 800, the Sticky Bomb will do around 75% of the health of any boss it sticks to, including the Hellborn with its lesser health.
  • The Spider will sometimes block the damage of the sticky bomb explosion if the bomb attached to one of its limbs, as the limbs can clip through walls.
  • The Sticky Bomb falls off of the Assassin when the boss dissolves into beetles.

Recommended Traits

The Sticky Bomb can use the following Traits:

  • Pitcher - Increased throwing range for all items using the aim helper.

Use Tips

Sticky Bombs have a longer fuse time than most other throwable explosives meaning if you want to detonate it as early as possible to the target, you will need to cook it for much longer. Sticky Bombs are best used for taking out priority targets as once stuck, they are garenteed to kill almost anything it sticks to. (barring bosses and some tough monsters.) Though sticking targets with it can take practice.

Book of Weapons

STICKY BOMB. (See also, EXPLOSIVES). The sticky bomb is a particularly cruel modified explosive device, comprised of two main parts: an outer frame and an inner charge. The inner charge employed here is most often a single stick of dynamite. This packs a significant punch, capable of blasting apart most objects and more than capable of incapacitating a person. When used in combat, a sole stick of dynamite suffers from one drawback: it is relatively unwieldy to throw and once landed may shift position, causing it to move away from its target. The sticky outer frame is a means to compensate for this, and adding a throwing handle ensures that the dynamite can be delivered with ease. The frame is mounted with crude metal hooks and spikes. If the frame hits a soft object with force, these will embed in the target, ensuring that the maximum power of the charge is delivered as close as possible.
