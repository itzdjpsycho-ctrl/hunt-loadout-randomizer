Description

An all-purpose tool and melee weapon that has saved many lives – and taken just as many.

Recommended Traits

The Knife can use the following Traits:

  • Silent Killer - Reduces the sound you make when performing melee attacks.

Use Tips

The knife is a solid overall option dealing reliable damage and it's piercing stab is great at dealing with specific monsters. Against players it is reliable and can even cause bleeding. However, like most piercing weapons or slashing weapons like the Heavy Knife it can cause immolators to ignite making it a risky option for dealing with them up close.

Book of Weapons

KNIFE. (See also, BLADED WEAPONS, TOOLS) At its most basic, a knife is a sharpened length of metal attached to a wooden handle, and variations on this design number in the thousands. The knife pictured here is a simple, functional hunting knife, designed for butchering game in the field—that is to say, skinning and boning an animal carcass. Because hunting knives are designed for cutting, they usually have a single sharp edge, with a slight curve at the tip. It can also be used, provisionally, for whittling, stabbing, slicing, and carving.
