Description

This portable tripwire mine releases firecrackers and a small flare, alerting others to its location. Comes in a pack of four.

Recommended Traits

The Alert Trip Mines can use the following Traits:

  • Poacher - Place and disarm traps quietly.
  • Vigilant - Nearby traps and active beetles are highlighted in Dark Sight. Also applies to Beetle view. (25m)

Use Tips

Alert Trips are great cheap tools when improvised. Setting them up close to Red and Yellow Barrels as well as other placable objects like Explosive Kegs can create potent traps for unsuspecting players. Stacking several together they can also help ignite players who trigger them.

Book of Weapons

ALERT TRIP MINE. (See also, TACTICAL DEVICES) An alert trip mine is an advanced device with a specific tactical application. While a non-fatal tool in function, the device is particularly suited to defensive tactics, constructed from two stakes with a taut wire strung between them. Each stake houses an additional firecracker and flare, which produce bright lights and loud sounds. Once the tripwire is snagged, they are ignited, surprising the unfortunate victim, and revealing their location to all in the surrounding area. When the flare drops back down to the ground, it illuminates the trip mine's site for a short time, making it particularly dangerous to remain in the area once one has been set off. This device was often homemade or improvised, being constructed out of common materials. Schematics for particular popular designs can be found, which are especially reliable.
