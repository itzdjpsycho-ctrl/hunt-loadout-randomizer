Description

A dynamite stick with a modified powder fuse that enables detonation under water and in choke bomb clouds.

Recommended Traits

The Waxed Dynamite Stick can use the following Traits:

  • Pitcher - Increased throwing range for all items using the aim helper.

Use Tips

Identical to the regular Dynamite Sticks but they cost slightly more in exchange for the benefit of working in wet enviroments and rain. If you find yourself on a lot of maps with water or rain they can be a worthwhile investment to your loadout.
