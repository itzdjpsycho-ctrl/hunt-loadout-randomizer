Description

Springfield made, bolt-action rifle. High fire-rate and ability to top off are offset by lower damage than other rifles. Modified with a silencer.

Recommended Traits

The Krag Silencer can use the following Traits:

  • Iron Eye - Remain in iron sights between shots when firing bolt-action, lever-action, and pump-action weapons.

Ammo Types

FMJ Ammo - 60

  • Drop Range: 130 → 120
  • Vertical Recoil: 3 → 6
  • Muzzle Velocity: 518 → 414
  • Damage dropoff begins at 50m

Incendiary Ammo - 70

  • Ignites Hunters in one shot up to 40m
  • Causes Medium Burning

Subsonic Ammo - 20

  • Ammo: 10 → 12
  • Drop Range: 130 → 105
  • Muzzle Velocity: 518 → 304
  • Reduced Sound
