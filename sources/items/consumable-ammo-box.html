Description

This small portable Ammo Box partially refills your weapons' extra ammunition, including special ammunition types.

Use Tips

Ammo Boxes can be good to bring when you and your team are using speical ammo types or weapons with limited ammo and can be espically handy when dealing with certain bosses such as scrapbeak who doesn't have any naturally spawning supplies. Just be wary when using it to resupply some weapons with Long Ammo, as the amount of ammo given is typically only a few rounds and may require multiple boxes to fill a magazine.

Book of Weapons

AMMO BOX (See also, AMMUNITION, STORAGE CONTAINERS) An ammo box is a portable container that is both fire retardant and weatherproofed to resist submersion in water, atmospheric humidity, and rain. These precautions are necessary to ensure the safe transportation of ammunition in hostile environments, where dampening the primer would cause ammunition to fail. The U.S. Army produced steel ammo boxes for standard issue; however, they could also be constructed with the provision of any watertight metal container, so long as the interior construction could prevent ammunition from rattling during transport.
