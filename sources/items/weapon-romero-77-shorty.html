Description

American made, break-action shotgun with single barrel. High damage, cost-effective, and tight bullet spread. Shortened stock and barrel.

Book of Weapons

Romero 77 SHORTY. (See also, SAWED-OFF SHOTGUNS) The Romero 77's utilitarian design—a single barrel and no magazine or reloading mechanism—made it simple to modify, the most common modification being a shortened barrel. This offers many advantages, most notably, greater mobility and easier handling, particularly advantageous in wooded and urban areas. The main drawback was losing the choke, making it less accurate at range. There was an additional psychological benefit to such modification, sawed-off shotguns are infamous with good reason.
