Description

Hunting Bow that fires arrows a great distance. Perfect for stealth. Arrows can be recovered and reused.

Information

  • An already drawn bow can be released again by using the Reload-Key
  • By default, light melee attacks will be performed with the fist, while heavy melee attacks will be performed with the wooden part of the bow
  • With the Trait Hundred Hands (see below), the heavy melee attacks will be performed with the arrow itself (only applies to Standard Arrows and Poison Arrows)

Recommended Traits

The Hunting Bow can use the following Traits:

  • Blade Seer - Bolts, Arrows, Throwing Axes, Throwing Knives, and Throwing Spears are highlighted in Dark Sight for better visibility. Also applies to Stalker Beetle View. (25m)
  • Hundred Hands - Increases the damage of a Hunting Bow and reduces sway while at full draw. Allows use of arrows in melee attacks.

Ammo Types

Concertina Arrows - 30

  • Extra Ammo: 6 → 4
  • Variable Damage: 250 → 152
  • Drop Range: 30 → 10
  • Spread: 12.5 → 40
  • Muzzle Velocity: 150 → 80
  • Causes Medium Bleeding
  • Creates a small bundle of concertina wire (6-8 strands) on impact

Frag Arrows - Scarce

  • Extra Ammo: 6 → 4
  • Variable Damage: 250 → 134
  • Drop Range: 30 → 10
  • Muzzle Velocity: 150 → 80
  • Causes Medium Bleeding
  • Explodes 2 seconds after release, causing bleeding
  • Note that the displayed damage given in-game is combined damage of minimum impact + splash damage, Frag Arrows have an impact damage from 25 to 50 depending on draw time and an maximum explosive damage of 111. Hundred Hands does not affect impact damage.

Poison Arrows - 25

  • Extra Ammo: 6 → 4
  • Spread: 12.5 → 30
  • Causes Intense Poison
  • Can be recovered and reused
  • Impacts deal 10 flat poison damage, while the total damage of the arrow is reduced to compensate the stats. This results in slightly higher overall damage, an extra 1m of one-shot range

Trivia

  • "Ochita Mozu" means "Fallen Shrike" in Japanese.
  • A hama yumi is a sacred bow, part of Japanese Buddhist and Shinto rituals of purification, and is named after the legend of an "Evil-Destroying Bow".
  • A raicho is a type of bird, otherwise known in Europe as a rock ptarmigan.

Book of Weapons

HUNTING BOW (See also, ARCHERY, BOW AND ARROW) is an instrument of hunting and ranged warfare intensively used for both purposes throughout the history of man. Although the size and shape can vary, a bow essentially consists of a precisely shaped stave that is bent when a string attached to its both ends is drawn back, producing enough tension to propel an arrow anywhere from fifteen to three hundred yards away.
