Description

Ludwig Arms made, semi-automatic pistol. High power and fire-rate. Incompatible with stripper clips. Modified with a Deadeye scope, but no stock. Can be dual wielded.

Recommended Traits

The Dolch 96 Bullseye can use the following Traits:

  • Bulletgrubber - Recover unfired round when performing partial reloads.
  • Quartermaster - Gain +1 Weapon Capacity
  • Steady Aim - Weapon sway gradually lessens when you're looking through a scope or Aperture sights.

Book of Weapons

DOLCH 96 BULLSEYE. (See also, GERMAN FIREARMS, PISTOL) It was vital that the scope modification to the Dolch 96 avoided hindering the pistol's ability to effortlessly holster. It was affixed slightly to the side of the pistol to not interfere with the action of the receiver. This way it can be wielded as a surprisingly dynamic weapon: a quick-firing sidearm that is effective at much longer distances than comparable pistols which aren't as lightweight.
