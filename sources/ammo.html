Regular Ammo

Ammo can be refilled in Ammo Crates, Ammo Boxes, and can be taken from the extra ammo pool of weapons laying on the ground so long as they are the same ammo size.

Compact Ammo

  • Basic pistol cartridge
  • Low penetration damage
  • Pierces thin wood sheets at close range
  • Cannot pierce stone and metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce trees

See all Compact Ammo weapons

Medium Ammo

  • High powered pistol cartridge
  • Moderate penetration damage
  • Pierces thin wood sheets at mid-range
  • Cannot pierce stone and metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce trees

See all Medium Ammo weapons

Long Ammo

  • Large caliber rifle cartridge
  • High penetration damage
  • Pierces wooden walls and small trees
  • Pierces thin stone walls and single metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce larger trees
  • Used for older rifle types with black powder cartridges

See all Long Ammo weapons

Shells

  • 12-Gauge buckshot
  • Low penetration damage
  • Pierces wooden walls at point-blank range
  • Cannot pierce stone and metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce trees

See all Shells weapons

Derringer

  • Pocket gun cartridge
  • Low damage
  • Pierces thin wood sheets at close range
  • Cannot pierce stone and metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce trees

Only used in the Quad Derringer, though it can replenish ammunition from Ammo Crates as normal.

Special Ammo

Special Ammo replenishes from Special Ammo Crates, Ammo Boxes, and can be taken from the extra ammo pool of weapons laying on the ground so long as they are of the same weapon family.

Arrow

  • Very strong damage
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters
  • No penetration
  • Sticks to target

Only used in the Hunting Bow.

Bolt

  • Very strong damage
  • No penetration
  • Sticks to target

Only used in the Crossbow and its variants.

Compact Bolt

  • Strong damage
  • No penetration
  • Sticks to target

Only used in the Hand Crossbow and Chu Ko Nu, but they do not share compatible ammo types despite sharing a name and description.

Special Medium Ammo

  • Modern smokeless pistol cartridge
  • Moderate penetration damage
  • Pierces thin wood sheets at mid-range
  • Cannot pierce stone and metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce trees

Only used in the Dolch 96 and its variants.

Special Long Ammo

  • Large caliber rifle cartridge
  • High penetration damage
  • Pierces wooden walls and small trees
  • Pierces thin stone walls and single metal sheets
  • Cannot pierce larger trees
  • Used for newer rifle types with smokeless powder cartridges

See all Special Long Ammo weapons

Lance Bolt

  • Very strong damage
  • Explodes After A Short Duration
  • No penetration
  • Sticks to target

Only used in the Bomb Launcher and Bomb Lance.

Nitro Ammo

  • Large bore hunting cartridge
  • Very strong penetration damage
  • Pierces multiple targets
  • Pierces stone walls and metal at mid-range
  • Pierces trees at mid-range

Only used in the Nitro Express.

Custom Ammo

Custom Ammo replenishes from Special Ammo Crates, Ammo Boxes, and can be taken from the extra ammo pool of weapons laying on the ground so long as they are of a compatible ammo size. Custom Ammo Boxes can replace a weapon's current ammo with the box's specific custom ammo if the weapon is capable of using it, or replenish the ammo if the weapon is already using it.

Dragon Breath

Description: "This shell creates a several-meter-long jet of flame that ignites anything in its path. Reduced range over buckshot."

  • Produces jet of flame
  • Reduced range
  • Destroys health chunks
  • No penetration

See all Dragon Breath weapons

Dumdum Ammo

Description: "These modified bullets expand and shatter on impact, causing severe bleeding, but at the expensive of penetration power and muzzle velocity. However, this additional stopping power has little effect on monsters and Infected."

  • Reduced muzzle velocity
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters
  • No penetration
  • Weak against monsters

See all Dumdum Ammo weapons

Explosive Ammo

Description: "This bullet contains a small charge that bursts on impact, scattering shrapnel over an area--the larger the bullet size, the greater the area. Reduced penetration, velocity, and damage, but a well-aimed shot can tear apart Grunts, Hives, and Hellhounds."

  • Reduced muzzle velocity
  • Explodes on impact
  • Minor explosive blast
  • Chance to ignite objects
  • Damage split between the explosion and the projectile
  • Destroys doors, shutters, and concertina

See all Explosive Ammo weapons

Flechette

Description: "This shell contains a number of smaller dart-like projectiles which fan out in a tight spread. Cannot pierce most surfaces, but can saturate an area and cause bleeding damage."

  • Fires multiple small darts
  • Reduced damage
  • Increased range
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters

See all Flechette weapons

FMJ Ammo

Description: "Full Metal Jacket (FMJ) bullets have a hardened casing that increases penetration power, though additional weight reduces muzzle speed. Better retains damage over distance."

  • Increased penetration
  • Decreased muzzle velocity
  • Decreased drop range

See all FMJ Ammo weapons

High Velocity Ammo

Description: "A hot-loaded bullet with a strong powder charge combined with a lighter projectile tip significantly boosts the muzzle velocity, but increases recoil slightly. Great for ranged combat."

  • Increased muzzle velocity
  • Increased drop range
  • Increased recoil
  • Decreased damage by 5%
  • Decreased reserve ammo

See all High Velocity Ammo weapons

Incendiary Ammo

Description: "This bullet contains a small phosphorous charge that ignites when fired and sets flammable targets alight. A visible trace can give the shooter's position away."

  • No penetration
  • Visible tracer
  • Hitting a hunter before damage drop off starts ignites them
  • Hititng a hunter with charred health ignites them regardless of range
  • Explodes red and yellow barrels
  • Ignites bosses for extra damage
  • Can destroy health chunks
  • Intensity increases over time

See all Incendiary Ammo weapons

Penny Shot Ammo

Description: "Coins replace shot pellets in this shell for a more powerful, if erratic, barrage. Shreds skin and flesh, but cannot penetrate harder obstacles. Unreliable at a distance."

  • Fires coins instead of pellets
  • Very high damage up-close
  • Highly unreliable at distance
  • No penetration of walls and barrels

See all Penny Shot Ammo weapons

Poison Ammo

Description: "This bullet shatters on impact, releasing a toxic agent fatal in high doses, but cannot penetrate surfaces."

  • Disorienting
  • Prevents health regeneration
  • Medium duration
  • Intensity increases through exposure

See all Poison Ammo weapons

Slug

Description: "Single solid projectile that can be used in shotguns to provide an advantage in range and penetration over Buckshot. Reduced ammunition. Becomes less accurate over distance."

  • Increased effective range
  • Reduced accuracy from hip
  • No extra head damage over distance
  • Single projectile

See all Slug weapons

Spitzer Ammo

Description: "Spitzer rounds revolutionize rifle ballistics, improving both penetration and muzzle velocity, though adding slightly stronger recoil. They are more stable in flight and can punch clean through multiple targets, but often cause less severe wounds."

  • Reduced damage
  • Increased penetration
  • Increased muzzle velocity
  • Increased recoil

See all Spitzer Ammo weapons

Starshell

Description: "A shell that launches a flare that descends slowly, illuminating the area. Ignites everything it touches."

  • Emits light
  • Blinding effect
  • Sets things ablaze
  • Can be used to distract

See all Starshell weapons

Subsonic Ammo

Description: "Smaller, weighted bullets with less gunpowder, which travel slower than the speed of sound for quieter gunshots."

  • Reduced sound
  • Reduced muzzle velocity
  • Reduced range

See all Subsonic Ammo weapons

Other Custom Ammo

Weapon families that use Special Ammo have custom ammo types that are not shared with other weapons. These are listed below.

Bomb Launcher / Bomb Lance

Dragon Breath Charge

Description: "This shell creates a several-meter-long jet of flame that ignites anything in its path."

  • Produces Jet Of Flame
  • Reduced Range
  • Destroys Health Chunks
  • No Penetration

Harpoon

Description: "A harpoon that causes bleeding damage and sticks to its target on impact. Can be retrieved after firing."

  • Strong damage
  • No penetration
  • Sticks to target

Steel Ball Ammo

Description: "A deadly burst of Steel Balls launched from a repurposed spring-loaded firing mechanism."

  • Silent
  • Very high damage up-close
  • Highly unreliable at distance
  • No penetration of walls and barrels

Waxed Frag Charge

Description: "Explosive lance with a protective wax coating, ideal for hunting creatures submerged in water."

  • Explosive blast
  • Delayed explosion on impact
  • Unaffected by water
  • Chance to ignite objects
  • No penetration of walls and barrels

Crossbow

Explosive Bolt

Description: "A highly volatile and explosive bolt."

  • Explosive blast
  • Explodes on impact
  • Chance To Ignite Objects
  • Reduced Velocity

Shot Bolt

Description: "Modified, multi-purpose bolts tipped with shotgun shells that detonates on impact."

  • 12-Gauge buckshot
  • Detonates on impact
  • Pierces wooden walls on impact
  • Cannot pierce stone and metal sheets

Steel Bolt

Description: "Metal bolts that fly faster and truer, making them more accurate at distance."

  • No penetration
  • Increased velocity
  • Sticks to target
  • Increased recoil

Chu Ko Nu

Compact Explosive Bolt

Description: "A highly volatile and explosive bolt."

  • Explosive blast
  • Explodes after a short duration
  • Chance to ignite objects
  • Reduced velocity

Compact Incendiary Bolt

Description: "A burning bolt that ignites its target, and is destroyed on impact. Cannot be retrieved."

  • Sets things ablaze
  • No penetration
  • Visible tracer

Hand Crossbow

Chaos Compact Bolt

Description: "A bolt that emulates the sound of a gunfight just like a Chaos Bomb, but with a shorter effect."

  • Emulates gunfire
  • Can distract enemies
  • Supports stealth tactics

Choke Compact Bolt

Description: "A Choke Bomb in the form of an arrow. Can be used offensively defensively, but has a smaller range."

  • Choke gas
  • Extinguishes fire and fuses
  • Causes coughing

Dragon Breath Compact Bolt

Description: "A compact Dragon Breath shell attached to a bolt, creating a jet of flame that explodes on impact."

  • Explodes on impact
  • Destroys health chunks
  • No penetration

Poison Compact Bolt

Description: "This bolt releases a highly toxic agent upon impact. Can cause death in seconds in high doses."

  • Poison damage
  • Disorienting
  • Prevents health regeneration
  • Lasting area effect

Revive Compact Bolt

Revive Compact Bolt ammo was removed from the game in Update 2.3.

Description: "A bolt for the Hand Crossbow that quickly revives a downed teammate on direct impact. Also heals wounded teammates on direct impact."

  • Revives downed teammates on impact
  • Heals alive teammates for 100hp
  • No penetration

Hunting Bow

Concertina Arrows

Description: "An arrow with a small Concertina Bomb attached to the point that explodes on impact."

  • Concertina wire
  • Explodes on impact
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters
  • Reduced velocity

Frag Arrows

Description: "An arrow with a small Frag Bomb attached to the point that will bounce on impact and detonate after a short time."

  • Explosive blast
  • Delayed explosion on impact
  • Chance to ignite objects
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters
  • Reduced velocity

Poison Arrows

Description: "A poison-coated arrow that can be retrieved and reused."

  • Very strong damage
  • Poison damage
  • Disorienting
  • Prevents health regeneration
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters
  • No penetration
  • Sticks to target

Nitro Express

Shredder Ammo

Description: "These modified bullets expand and shred on impact, causing severe bleeding. They do not lose all of their penetration power, and do lose some muzzle velocity."

  • Reduced muzzle velocity
  • Causes bleeding on Hunters
  • Reduced penetration
  • Weak against monsters
