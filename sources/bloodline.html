Bloodline

From Hunt: Showdown 1896 Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

The Bloodline is the primary progression system in Hunt: Showdown 1896. Players start at Rank 1 and advance until Rank 100 through earning Bloodline . Every rank unlocks either new Tools, Consumables or Traits or grants some Hunt Dollars.

Bloodline can be gained in various ways:

  • Advancing a Hunter (see Hunter Progression): certain Hunter levels grant a Bloodline bonus
  • Retiring a Hunter at level 50 (grants 10,000 Bloodline )
  • Dark Tribute reward
  • Looting Bloodline XP Envelopes (grants 500 Bloodline )

If the player reaches Bloodline Rank 100, he has the option to Prestige.

Trainee Mode

Trainee mode is a special status applied to each new player and is removed when the player first reaches Bloodline Rank 15. In Trainee mode, a player's Hunter is not lost (see Permadeath) if it gets killed or fails to extract during a Bounty Hunt or Bounty Clash mission.

Bloodline Tiers

By progressing through the Bloodline the player also advances its Bloodline Tier. Tier I reaches from Rank 1 to 33, Tier II from Rank 34 to 66 and Tier III from Rank 67 to 100.

Before Update 2.0, this Tier was important for non-legendary Hunter recruitment. Now it is only used for the initial Steam and console achievements of reaching those levels for the first time.
