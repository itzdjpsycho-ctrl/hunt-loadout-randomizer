Blademancer
Category
Supportive
Type
Scarce 

While using Dark Sight, retrievable Ammo in front of you returns automatically to your inventory. (100m)

Update History

Update Patch Notes
Post Malone's Murder Circus Event Blademancer added as an Event Trait for the Trickshooter Troupe - "Using Dark Sight, take retrievable ammo in front of you. Does damage to enemies if stuck in them. Runes on Sawblades prevent them from being retrieved. (100m)"
Garden of the Witch Event Blademancer added as an Event Trait , costing 2 . Damage on pullout removed.

Update 2.9 Blademancer added as a Scarce Trait, found in Scout Towers and Special Supply Points
