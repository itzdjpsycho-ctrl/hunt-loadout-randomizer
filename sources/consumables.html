Consumables

From Hunt: Showdown 1896 Wiki
Jump to navigation Jump to search

Consumables make up the secondary utility of a Hunter's loadout, giving powerful one-time effects such as healing shots or thrown bombs. Some Consumables are unlocked at the start, while the rest are unlocked through Bloodline progression. Each Consumable can only be used once, at which point it disappears from the 4-slot inventory and can be replaced by another.

In Bounty Hunt and Bounty Clash, players equip a Hunter with their desired loadout of Consumables, Weapons, and Tools before the match, then spawn in with them.
In Soul Survivor, players have to search the map for Consumables to equip and utilize.

In both modes, Consumables can also be looted from Item Boxes, Downed Hunters, Saddlebags, Hunting Towers, and Watch Towers.

You can alphabetically see all Tools by visiting the Consumables Category.

Tarot Cards

Tarot Cards are single-use Consumables with unique abilities based on their traditional meanings, first introduced in Update 2.5 during the Web of the Empress Event.

All cards are Scarce, meaning they cannot be bought with Hunt Dollars . Instead, they can be collected in the following ways:

  • Finding them in the world: 1 random Tarot Card is available in each Bileweaver Infested Compound, and supply points such as the Postal Supply, Clockmaker's Supply, Sealed Hoard, and Event-Specific Points of Interest spawn either specific or random Tarot Cards.
  • Collecting Dark Tribute rewards: 1 Tarot Card is earned at each Dark Tribute stage.
  • Looting them from dead Hunters: Any Tarot Cards carried by dead Hunters can be stolen when looting, so long as you have a free Consumable slot for them.

Inspecting a card during a match will cause a small hint pop-up to appear on the top right of the screen detailing the equipped Tarot Card’s effect.

All Tarot Cards can be sold for 150 each, even after retrieving them from a mission.

Consumable Statistics

Consumable Stats of the Dynamite Stick

Consumable Stats quantify the qualities of each Consumable. Unlike weapons, these stats are hardly modified, except for the Pitcher trait extending Throw Range.

Damage

  • Damage value of a consumable hitting a Hunter with its full effect.

Damage per Tick

  • Damage while affected by a consumable

Effect Duration

  • The time a consumable effect lasts in seconds.

Effect Radius

  • Effect radius in meters.

Fuse Timer

  • The time (in seconds) until a consumable detonates

Throw Range

  • The furthest range (in meters) a consumable can be thrown.

Control Range

  • The maximum range (in meters) with which Stalker Beetles and its variants can be used.

Melee Damage

  • Damage value of a light or heavy melee attack that hits a Hunter in the upper torso

Stamina Consumption

  • The stamina consumed (out of 100) with a light or heavy melee attack
