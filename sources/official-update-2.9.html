Update 2.9 Patch Notes

[Patch Introduction]

Blood Moon Time of Day

A new and dynamic Time of Day—or rather, time of night—rises over the Incursion areas and shifts between two phases throughout your Mission. During the Intense Blood Moon phase, a storm grows and the moon breaks through the clouds. Monsters become more aggressive and attack faster. During the Dark Blood Moon phase, clouds cover the moon, reducing visibility and returning monster behavior to normal. Both phases bathe the map in a red glow, with the effect becoming stronger and brighter when the Blood Moon leaves its cloud cover.

[Highlights]

New weapon: Burgess

The Burgess is a foldable, slide-action, long barrel shotgun with a Weapon Capacity of Size 3 at the cost of a long equip and unequip animation. It features a 6-round magazine. It can be equipped with the “Bulletgrubber" Trait to recover ejected live shells.

  • Basic - 6+1/6
  • Dragon Breath - 6+1/6
  • Flechette - 6+1/6
  • Penny Shot - 6+1/9
  • Slugs - 6+1/4

New variant: Burgess Bayonet

A variant of the Burgess that has a bayonet added to it, which comes at the cost of an even longer equip and unequip animation, as it takes more time to deploy and stow away.

  • Basic - 6+1/6
  • Dragon Breath - 6+1/6
  • Flechette - 6+1/6
  • Penny Shot - 6+1/9
  • Slugs - 6+1/4

New variant: Burgess Trauma

A variant of the Burgess with a reinforced stock that changes the heavy melee attack into a hammer strike.

  • Basic - 6+1/6
  • Dragon Breath - 6+1/6
  • Flechette - 6+1/6
  • Penny Shot - 6+1/9
  • Slugs - 6+1/4

Dev note:

During Blood Testament, all Burgess variants are unlocked via the Battle Pass and will stay unlocked over Prestige. Once Blood Testament ends, they will be added to normal weapon progression.

Gameplay

Hidden Information Changes

  • All Supply Points, Scout Towers, and Extraction Points are hidden by default.
  • Investigating a Clue will now reveal 2 Extraction Points.
  • The Scouting Maps that reveal hidden info can now appear at all Supply Points.
  • Scout Towers still have a guaranteed Scouting Map inside.
  • The Spyglass still reveals hidden location icons on the compass.
  • The radius to discover hidden locations has been increased to 75m.

Dev Note: We've taken note of community feedback and hope this alleviates some of the pain points of the previous iterations of the hidden info mechanic. We hope this adds focus to the core game loop, while adding the element off discovery by keeping a lot of information hidden.

Trait Changes

  • New Burn Trait: Siphon - Allows you to loot a Scarce Trait off an enemy Hunter's body, leaving Siphon in its place. Siphon can be acquired by interacting with Blood Wells or found in-world like other Scarce Traits.

Dev Note: Siphon will prioritize Death Cheat whenever possible, and if not, it will steal a random Scarce Trait if the looted Hunter had one. For solos, if not Death Cheat, it will then prioritize Necromancer, but you will not be able to steal Necromancer from another solo player, only from someone in a team. When Death Cheat is stolen, it will still be active for the player that lost it for the rest of the round.

  • New Burn Trait: Chupacabra - Interact with a live horse, cow, or animal cage to restore 1 Health Chunk.

Dev Note: Once unlocked, Chupacabra will be buyable from the Trait menu for 2 Upgrade Points. When interacting with dog cages, all 3 dogs can be interacted with separately, while for chickens the entire cage can be interacted with once. The chosen animal is triggered during the interaction and killed upon completion.

Catalyst Updates:

  • Vulture: Now gets a Catalyst Conditional which allows players to loot some ammo from downed Hunters.
  • Lightfoot: Now gets the Catalyst Conditional to never break branches. This effect was previously part of the Catalyst Conditionals for Beastface, but has now been moved to Lightfoot.

Trait Spawn Changes:

  • Added Crackshot, Blademancer, and Corpse Seer to the list of possible Trait spawns at Scout Towers and themed Supply Points. Both Sealed and normal Trait Spur variations can spawn.


Misc. Gameplay Changes

  • Destiny's Dial now has a chance to reveal all hidden locations as a reward.

Sealed Reward Spawn Changes:

  • Postal Supply now only contains the Experience Boon, with the other Boons moving to more thematically appropriate Supply Points.
  • Clockmaker's Supply now contains a Dark Sight Boon.
  • Reduced the chance of Sealed Boons to spawn in Scout Towers and Supply Points.


Audio

  • Added a short variation of the Rise Up Dead Man “Humming Theme" to play after a successful extraction.
  • Fixed an issue with certain Equipment Animation sounds in first person to be heard after canceling the inspect.


Monsters

  • The Intense Blood Moon phase from the “Blood Moon" Time of Day affects monsters except for Water Devils.

o Triggered by the Blood Moon (general effect in all maps) or when players interact with Blood Wells for the Community Event (both in a radius for a short time).

o Agitated monsters have the following Traits:

 A dark, smoky aura.

 They sound more aggressive.

 They have slightly sharper senses while roaming.

 They move faster. Armoreds and Leeches are most affected by the change, while Hellhounds and Immolators retain their original movement speed.

 Grunts, Armoreds, and Firebreathers (when wet) attacks occur at shorter intervals, so they deal more damage over time.

 Attack animations for Grunts, Armoreds, Firebreathers (when wet), Brutes, Ursa Mortises, and Meatheads are faster.

  • Immolator navigation has been nerfed, no longer allowing the monster to figure out long, complex paths to players. Detection distances remain the same, preserving the experience in open fields where the Immolator has a straight path to its target.
  • Hellhounds are now slower when they are in water or have a mutilated leg.


New Player Onboarding

  • Low visibility Time of Day rates have been reduced for new players to ease their first steps in the game.


UI

  • Traits in Loadouts

o A much-requested feature is finally here! You are now able to add Traits to Custom Loadouts and, in the process, hopefully cut down on the number of steps to re-equip a Hunter after death.

 Custom Loadouts now come with a dual cost: Hunt Dollars and Upgrade Points.

 You can equip 0-15 Traits to a loadout. Cost is replaced with total budget needed.

 The cost of the Traits will be applied when you equip the Loadout to your Hunter.

 If you have insufficient Upgrade Points, you will not be allowed to equip the Loadout and will be shown an error message.

 You can still copy your Hunter's loadout into a Custom Loadout slot.

 Scarce and Locked Traits cannot be copied over to a Custom Loadout from a Hunter. Those items will be skipped once the action is taken.

 You can disable the Traits widget if you want to equip the Custom Loadout without the Traits.

  • Hunter Slots

o Expanded the total possible number of Hunter Slots from 75 to 100.

  • Made improvements to the localization for French, Russian, Chinese Simplified, and German.


Matchmaking

  • MMR Decay

o Returning players will now receive a temporary MMR modifier that slightly reduces difficulty of Missions after a longer break from the game.

o This modifier gradually decreases the more you play, perfect for when you are feeling rusty and need a few games to get back on track.

  • New Player Modifier

o Before, this was a flat-value MMR reduction that was turned off at specific Bloodline Rank, resulting in a spike of difficulty for new players.

o Now it uses similar logic to MMR Decay—a modifier that smoothly increases difficulty and gives newcomers a fair fighting chance against more experienced (but similarly skilled MMR-wise) players.
