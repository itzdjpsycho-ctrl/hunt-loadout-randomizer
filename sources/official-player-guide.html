Welcome to the fight against Corruption, Hunter.

The journey ahead is sure to bring your fair share of adrenaline rushes and dark discoveries. For new players, we’d like to offer some advice on how to survive our signature game mode, Bounty Hunt. From building your loadout to navigating the Mission itself, this player guide features the basics of what to expect when it comes to investigating Clues, monster types, and more. We hope the information will help you better your chances to successfully extract the Bounty and live to die another day.

Good luck out there.


Step 1

Pick a Hunter for Bounty Hunt

After the Tutorial, navigate to the "Hunters" tab to get an overview about your available Hunters.

Step 2

Recruit and Choose a Hunter for a Mission

There are a few Hunters already recruited in your Roster. Three are beginner Hunters who are Bloodline Rank 1 and have a loadout already equipped. One of them will be a higher Bloodline Rank—this is the Hunter you played in the Tutorial, and they still have all the equipment that you extracted with. Select any one of these Hunters to get an overview of their equipment and Traits.

Alternatively: Click on one of the two "+" symbols to either recruit an available Legendary Hunter for 100 Hunt Dollars or another free Hunter who comes with some basic equipment.

Step 3

Equip Your Hunter

There are two sections where you can equip your Hunter here: one with Weapons, Tools, and Consumables, and one with Traits.

Clicking on either of those sections brings you to a loadout screen for equipment and Traits.
For your loadout, there are two categories:

  • Equipment Slots – Aside from eight total Tools and Consumables, your Hunter can also equip two Weapons, which share a pool of Weapon Capacity. Your Hunter starts with a maximum Weapon Capacity of 5. This means that if you choose to equip a Size 3 Weapon, you can then decide between equipping a Size 1 or 2 as a secondary, so as not to exceed the maximum limit of 5.
    Tip: The “Quartermaster” Trait raises your Weapon Capacity from 5 to 6.
  • Trait Slots – Traits can be purchased for your Hunter using Upgrade Points. Traits are special abilities which grant things like added support for certain types of weapons, healing yourself or your teammates, resistance to Status Effects, and many more.

For an overview of all the Traits in the game, click here.

Step 4

Loadout Suggestions and Trait Tips for Beginners

Here are some examples of beginner loadouts:

Each of these loadouts are built for a different range, and work whether you are playing solo or with a team.

For Traits, the “Necromancer” and “Determination” Traits are both excellent choices for beginner Hunters. For the cost of 4 Upgrade Points, Necromancer enables you to revive a downed teammate from a distance one time, or, if you're solo, it grants you the ability to revive yourself. With Determination, your stamina will start regenerating faster once depleted. Both Traits can offer critical assistance in tight situations.

Step 5

Choose Your Mission Rules

You can adjust the specifics of team sizes here. Under "My Team," choose to either play as a solo, search for random teammates to form a duo or trio, or use the "+" buttons on either side of your Hunter avatar to invite friends to play with you. Under "Opposing Teams," if you are playing solo or in a duo, you can either keep the maximum number of players per team at two or select trios to raise it to three.

Once the team sizes are set, your Hunter is equipped (don't forget your First Aid Kit!), and you are ready, click the “READY TO PLAY” button to start your first real Bounty Hunt. Good luck out there, Hunter!

For an overview of all possible Times of Day, click here.

Step 1

Locate Your First Clue

After loading into the Mission, activate Dark Sight to locate Clues. Clues are highlighted as clouds of little floating blue dots.

You will come up against many monsters while looking for Clues and moving around the map. For an overview of all the types of monsters you’ll encounter, click here.

Step 2

Track Down the Boss Target

After interacting with a Clue, open your map. Compounds where the Boss Target is NOT located will be grayed out, narrowing down its potential location. Every team in the Mission will experience the same order of map gray-outs as they investigate Clues, regardless of where the Clues are located. If there are two Boss Targets present in the Mission, the map will be visibly divided; any Clues found within each section will only help investigate the Boss Target dwelling in that same section of the map.

Step 3

Revealing a Boss Target Lair

You can reveal the exact location of the Boss Target Lair by either investigating three Clues, stumbling into the Boss Target Lair by accident, or spending Pledge Marks to investigate a Master Clue (Master Clues are not available in every Mission). The final compound left highlighted on the map is where the Boss Target is located.

Step 4

Kill and Banish a Boss Target

Once you're within 50 meters of the Boss Target Lair, you can activate Dark Sight to check the Whisper status of the area. If the outline of the Boss Target icon on the top left corner of your screen is flashing white, it means there are no enemy Hunters within the defined perimeter. If the icon is flashing red, however, it means that you’re not alone and there is at least one enemy Hunter nearby.

Each Boss Target has its own individual abilities, weaknesses, and strengths. For an overview of all the different Boss Targets, click here.

Step 5

Pick Up a Bounty Token

Picking up a Bounty Token provides you with five seconds of Dark Sight Boost and as well as an effect called Instinct. Instinct allows you to detect nearby enemy Hunters—but it also reveals your name and position to every other Hunter on the map.

The Instinct effect is only activated while in Dark Sight. It can be seen as an orange aura that appears around the perimeter of your screen whenever there is an enemy Hunter within a 75-meter radius of you. If you have a Bounty Token but do not see the orange effect while in Dark Sight, you’re alone…for now.

Holding right-click while in Dark Sight activates your Dark Sight Boost, revealing all enemy Hunters within your visual range of up to 125 meters as blurry orange outlines. Use it carefully—you only have five seconds total! You can choose to use your Dark Sight Boost at any pace you’d like. It is not required to use all 5 seconds at once.

Step 6

Extract the Bounty

Once you've acquired the Bounty, you can head for an Extraction Point. Once you extract, the Bounty will be converted into Hunt Dollars and experience. Check your map for Extraction Point locations. If an Extraction Point has a lock symbol over it on the map, it is currently locked and can't be used to extract unless “The Chariot” Tarot Card is used first.

Extraction takes thirty seconds to complete. If you or one of your teammates is downed during the countdown, the timer will freeze until the downed person is revived. The timer will also freeze if an enemy Hunter enters the Extraction Point with you, effectively blocking your extract.

After the countdown is complete, your Hunter will extract, and the Mission is officially over.

Step 7

After the Extract

After Extraction, you will see a Mission Summary screen that sums up your accomplishments on the Mission, including the number of Bounty collected, the number of monsters and enemy Hunters killed, the progression gained in your Bloodline Rank, and total rewards earned.

From the Mission Summary, you can choose to either return directly to the Lobby or click one of the two options on the left side of the screen, which will allow you to access either Summary Details or Accolade Cards.

Summary Details will reveal more information on the teams you played against in the Mission. Accolade Cards will highlight all individual accomplishments achieved.

And now you can begin the entire loop again.

Breakdown of Weapons

The weapons in Hunt: Showdown 1896 use a range of different ammo types, including Compact, Medium, Long, Special Long, Shells, Bolts, Arrows, Special, and several types of Custom Ammo. Each weapon has its own Weapon Capacity Size; you can equip your loadout to contain a maximum capacity of 5.

Breakdown of Traits

Traits enhance the abilities and skills of your Hunter, from improving your usage of specific weapons, over status condition resistance to improved healing skills.

Adrenaline
Receive a small Stamina boost when entering critical health.
Ambidextrous
Quicker reloading of dual-wielded pistols, and custom clip reloads for semi-auto pistol sets.
Assailant
Increase melee effectiveness of Throwing Knives and Throwing Axes.
Blast Sense
While using Dark Sight, loud sounds such as gunshots and explosions are highlighted. Gunshots from silenced weapons with Subsonic Ammo can't be seen.
Bloodless
Bleeding will not escalate from light to medium or intense.
Bolt Thrower
Reduce reload time for Crossbows, Bomb Launchers, and Bomb Lances.
Bulletgrubber
Recover unfired round when performing partial reloads.
Bulwark
Reduce the damage taken from explosions by 50%.

Breakdown of Maps

Hunt: Showdown 1896 currently has four maps: Stillwater Bayou, Lawson Delta, DeSalle, and Mammon’s Gulch, all of which feature unique compounds and designs.

Breakdown of Status Effects

There are both positive and negative Status Effects in Hunt: Showdown 1896. Some of them do things like drain your Health or keep it from regenerating, while others can offer infinite Stamina or grant immunity against Poison or Fire Damage.

Breakdown of Targets and Monsters

The Targets in Hunt: Showdown 1896 provide the main source of income in the game. Tracking and killing them rewards you with the Bounty Tokens that will convert to Hunt Dollars if you manage to extract them without dying.

While tracking your Target, you will come across a range of different types of monsters scattered throughout the map who will try to stop you.
