# Traits in the Chaos app

Checked 23 September 2026. Switches mean the selected Hunter already has the trait. They do not purchase it, unlock weapons, or increase any inventory limit unless the trait explicitly changes that limit. At most 15 can be selected.

Quartermaster changes the shared weapon budget from 5 to 6. Frontiersman changes limited-use tool benefits, including pocket-pistol reserve magazines. Two extra uses require both solo play and Catalyst. Doctor changes the First Aid Kit's displayed healing to 100 per use; Physician affects medkit use speed.

Other supported trait switches affect equipment weighting and benefit labels. They are optional preferences, not requirements to equip the gear. Compatible equipment is usually identified from each item page's Recommended Traits section, restricted to the supported trait list. Explicit current trait statements override older item recommendations:

- Fanning includes compatible stock-equipped single-action pistols under its current description, but excludes LeMat carbines and double-action weapons.
- Fast Fingers includes the 1890 Cavalry, confirmed by the official 2.8 release notes.
- Poacher excludes Dark Dynamite Satchels even though the satchel's older recommended-traits section lists it.
- Assailant applies to Throwing Knives/Axes, not the Throwing Spear.
- Quartermaster is removed from per-item synergy lists because it is a loadout capacity rule.

The app explains Frontiersman in terms of extra uses; it does not claim to supply a complete base-charge inventory model. Ammo pools, reload speeds and damage simulations are outside this app's scope. Ambidextrous is omitted while dual-wield generation is disabled. General defensive and movement traits do not change equipment eligibility and are not part of the loadout-selection controls.
